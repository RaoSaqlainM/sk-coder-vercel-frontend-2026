# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 15, 2026

## 1. Introduction

SK Coder ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") operates the SK Coder web application and mobile platforms (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service, including any related applications, websites, and other services we provide.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Service. By accessing and using SK Coder, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by all the provisions of this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Information:** When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, username, password, and profile information
- **Project Data:** Code files, project configurations, and any other content you upload or create within SK Coder are stored in your workspace
- **Communication Data:** When you contact our support team, we store your messages, attachments, and related information
- **Payment Information:** If applicable, payment details are processed through secure third-party payment processors. We do not store complete payment information

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Device Information:** OS type, browser type, IP address, and unique device identifiers
- **Usage Data:** Features accessed, functions performed, time and duration of activities, and interaction data
- **Cookies and Similar Technologies:** We use cookies and web beacons to track your activity and remember your preferences
- **Performance Data:** App performance metrics, crash reports, and error logs to improve our Service

### 2.3 Third-Party Integrations

- **GitHub Integration:** If you authorize SK Coder to access your GitHub account, we receive and store your GitHub username, repositories, and OAuth tokens
- **AI API Integration:** If you provide your own API keys (OpenAI, Google Gemini, etc.), we process requests through these services per their terms
- **Backend Services:** Execution logs and temporary data may be stored on our Oracle Cloud servers for code execution purposes

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:
- Provide and maintain the SK Coder Service
- Execute your code on our backend infrastructure
- Improve features, performance, and user experience
- Debug and troubleshoot issues
- Authenticate your identity and prevent fraud
- Comply with legal obligations
- Send service updates and important notices
- Respond to your inquiries and support requests
- Analyze usage patterns to develop new features

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

- **Project Files:** Stored indefinitely in your workspace (IndexedDB locally, optional cloud backup)
- **Execution Cache:** Temporary files on Oracle EC2 are auto-deleted after 72 hours
- **AI Chat History:** Stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB
- **GitHub Tokens:** Stored securely in browser local storage; never transmitted to SK Coder servers unless you explicitly enable cloud sync
- **Backend Logs:** Retained for 30 days for debugging and optimization

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT share your personal data with third parties except:
- **Service Providers:** Hosting providers (Oracle Cloud), CDN services, analytics platforms that support our operations
- **Law Enforcement:** When legally required by valid court orders or government requests
- **Business Transfers:** In case of merger, acquisition, or sale of SK Coder assets
- **API Providers:** Your code and queries may be sent to third-party execution services (Piston, Wandbox) based on your language selection
- **GitHub:** If you authorize integration, limited data is shared for repository operations

## 6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:
- **SSL/TLS Encryption:** All data in transit is encrypted
- **Local Storage:** Your workspace files are stored locally in your browser initially
- **Backend Security:** Secure authentication, rate limiting, and access controls on our servers
- **Regular Audits:** We conduct security reviews and penetration testing

However, no security system is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. User Rights and Choices

### 7.1 Access and Portability
You have the right to:
- Access your personal data and request a copy
- Export your projects and data as ZIP files
- Receive your data in a machine-readable format

### 7.2 Correction and Deletion
You can:
- Update your account information through Settings
- Delete your workspace and associated data at any time
- Request complete account deletion (within 30 days of request, all data is purged)

### 7.3 Opt-Out Options
You can opt-out of:
- Analytics and usage tracking
- Marketing emails (no marketing emails are sent currently)
- Optional cloud backup features

## 8. Children's Privacy

For users between 13-18, parental consent may be required in certain jurisdictions.

## 12. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have specific rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):
- **Right to Know:** What personal information is collected, used, and shared
- **Right to Delete:** Request deletion of your personal information
- **Right to Opt-Out:** Opt-out of personal information sales (we do not sell personal information)
- **Right to Non-Discrimination:** We do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights

To exercise these rights, contact us at: **privacy@skcoder.app**

## 13. GDPR Compliance (EU/EEA Users)

If you are located in the EU or EEA, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):
- **Right to Access:** Obtain copies of your personal data
- **Right to Rectification:** Correct inaccurate personal data
- **Right to Erasure:** Request deletion of your personal data (right to be forgotten)
- **Right to Restrict Processing:** Limit how we process your data
- **Right to Portability:** Receive your data in a portable format
- **Right to Lodge a Complaint:** File a complaint with your local data protection authority
